💚 Welcome to Envy Roleplay

Envy Roleplay is focused on realistic, entertaining and story-driven roleplay. By playing on Envy Roleplay, you agree to follow all server and community rules.

PLAYER RESPONSIBILITY
Every player is responsible for knowing the rules. Not knowing a rule does not automatically excuse a violation.

Good RP > Winning.

01 • General Rules

1.1 Respect

Treat players and staff with respect. Harassment, excessive toxicity, discrimination, targeted bullying and threats are prohibited.

1.2 Cheating

Mod menus, aimbots, ESP, wall hacks, unauthorized combat modifications or any software providing an unfair advantage are prohibited.

Cheating may result in an immediate permanent ban.

1.3 Exploiting

Do not intentionally abuse bugs, duplicate money or items, exploit inventories, glitch through walls or abuse unintended game mechanics.

1.4 Ban Evasion

Using another account or identity to bypass a punishment is prohibited.

1.5 Staff Interactions

Follow staff instructions during administrative situations. Disagreements should be handled through tickets or appeals.

1.6 Common Sense

Not every possible situation can be written into the rules. Use reasonable judgment and prioritize good roleplay.

02 • Roleplay Rules

2.1 Stay In Character

Remain in character during active RP. Do not discuss tickets, staff reports, Discord conversations or other OOC matters during scenes.

2.2 FailRP

Unrealistic behavior that significantly damages an RP situation may be considered FailRP.

2.3 FearRP / Value Your Life

Your character must reasonably value their life. React appropriately when another player clearly has control of a dangerous situation.

2.4 Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic actions or outcomes onto another player.

2.5 Metagaming

Information obtained through Discord, streams, DMs, friends or OOC conversations cannot be used by your character.

2.6 Stream Sniping

Using someone's livestream to locate them or gain information is prohibited.

2.7 Character Separation

Knowledge and relationships should not automatically transfer between your characters.

03 • RDM / VDM & Combat

3.1 RDM

Randomly attacking or killing another player without sufficient RP is prohibited.

3.2 VDM

Improperly using a vehicle as a weapon against another player is prohibited.

3.3 Combat Initiation

Clear RP interaction must establish hostility before an attack.

3.4 Revenge Killing

Do not immediately hunt down someone because they previously killed your character.

3.5 Combat Logging

Disconnecting to avoid arrest, robbery, death, losing items, kidnapping or a police pursuit is prohibited.

3.6 Third Partying

You must have a legitimate RP connection before entering an active conflict.

04 • Criminal Rules

4.1 Criminal RP

Criminal activity should create roleplay rather than simply creating shootouts.

4.2 Player Robberies

A legitimate RP interaction must exist before robbing another player.

4.3 Robbery Cooldown

Wait at least 30 minutes before beginning another major robbery.

4.4 Hostages

Hostages must be legitimate. Friends or gang members may not be used as fake hostages to manipulate police.

4.5 Maximum Criminals

A maximum of 6 criminals may actively participate in one organized criminal situation unless otherwise approved.

4.6 Cop Baiting

Intentionally provoking police solely to create a chase or shootout is prohibited.

05 • Gang Rules

5.1 Registration

Official gangs must be approved by server management.

5.2 Gang Limits

Official gangs may have up to 12 registered members.

A maximum of 6 gang members may participate in one hostile situation.

5.3 Gang Wars

Gang wars require a legitimate RP reason. OOC disagreements are not valid reasons.

5.4 Territories

Owning territory does not allow a gang to randomly attack players entering the area.

5.5 Alliances

Gang alliances may not be used to bypass participation limits.

06 • Police / EMS Rules

6.1 Professional Conduct

Police and EMS are expected to maintain a higher standard of RP.

6.2 Police Corruption

Police corruption requires management approval.

6.3 Department Equipment

Restricted department equipment may not be given or sold to civilians without approval.

6.4 Use of Force

Police should use force appropriate to the threat.

6.5 EMS Neutrality

On-duty EMS must remain neutral during criminal conflicts.

6.6 Scene Safety

EMS should not knowingly enter an active shootout until the scene is reasonably safe.

07 • Vehicle & Pursuit Rules

7.1 Realistic Driving

Drive according to the capabilities of your vehicle.

7.2 Vehicle FailRP

Unrealistic jumps, driving ordinary vehicles up mountains or continuing after catastrophic vehicle damage may be considered FailRP.

7.3 Pursuits

Police and criminals should prioritize entertaining pursuit RP.

7.4 Ramming

Repeated unrealistic vehicle ramming may be considered FailRP or VDM.

7.5 Vehicle Swapping

Do not repeatedly switch vehicles solely to abuse mechanics or artificially extend a pursuit.

08 • Death / New Life Rule

8.1 NLR

After fully dying and respawning, you may not immediately return to the situation that caused your death.

8.2 NLR Timer

Do not return to the immediate scene for at least 15 minutes.

8.3 Memory

After fully respawning, your character should not remember the exact circumstances immediately surrounding their death.

8.4 Returning

You may not return to assist friends still involved in the situation or immediately recover dropped property.

09 • Business Rules

9.1 Activity

Player-owned businesses should remain reasonably active.

9.2 Economy Abuse

Business systems may not be exploited to generate unintended money or items.

9.3 Employees

Business owners are responsible for reasonably managing employees.

9.4 Business Funds

Business accounts should primarily be used for legitimate business purposes.

10 • Green Zones

Hospitals, government buildings, spawn locations and other locations announced by staff may be designated Green Zones.

10.1 Starting Crime

Do not intentionally begin violent criminal activity inside a Green Zone.

10.2 Escaping

You may not enter a Green Zone simply to escape an active RP situation.

10.3 Existing RP

A Green Zone does not automatically cancel legitimate RP that began elsewhere.

11 • Discord Rules

11.1 Respect

Treat community members respectfully.

11.2 Spam

Do not spam messages, mentions, images or voice channels.

11.3 Advertising

Advertising other FiveM servers without management approval is prohibited.

11.4 NSFW Content

NSFW or excessively graphic content is prohibited.

11.5 Tickets

Use support tickets for legitimate issues and player reports.

12 • Punishments

Possible punishments include:

🟢 Verbal Warning
🟡 Written Warning
🟠 Kick
🔴 Temporary Ban
⛔ Permanent Ban

Cheating, malicious exploiting, serious ban evasion or intentionally attempting to damage the server may result in an immediate permanent ban.

13 • RP Terminology

RDM

Random Deathmatch — attacking or killing without sufficient RP.

VDM

Vehicle Deathmatch — improperly using a vehicle as a weapon.

NLR

New Life Rule.

FailRP

Unrealistic behavior that damages roleplay.

FearRP

Reasonably valuing your character's life.

Metagaming

Using OOC information in character.

Powergaming

Forcing unrealistic actions or abusing mechanics.

Combat Logging

Disconnecting to escape an active RP situation.

OOC

Out of Character.

IC

In Character.

📹 Player Reports

1. Continue RP when reasonably possible.
2. Save a clip or recording.
3. Get the player's server ID/name.
4. Open a Discord support ticket.
5. Explain what happened and provide evidence.

Do not threaten players with staff action during RP.

🛡️ Staff Rules

Staff members may not abuse permissions, spawn items for personal advantage, give friends preferential treatment, use staff information during RP or punish players because of personal disagreements.

💚 Final Rule

ROLEPLAY OVER WINNING

You will not win every chase.
You will not win every gunfight.
You will not escape every arrest.
You will not successfully complete every robbery.

Losing a situation does not mean the RP was bad.

Good RP > Winning.
